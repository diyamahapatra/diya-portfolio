Overview

Students had to understand how UC Berkeley’s advising is structured before they could get help from it.

L&S is Berkeley’s largest college, and its advising site supports everything from appointments to petitions and deadlines. But students had to navigate unclear advising distinctions, hidden options, and buried eligibility requirements just to figure out where to go for help.

I redesigned three high-priority advising experiences, a routing flow, and the component system behind them, grounded in 261 survey responses and usability testing with nine students. Rather than explaining Berkeley’s advising structure more clearly, the designs reduce how much of that structure students need to understand in the first place.

Design System

A 90+ component system with best-practice standards, built through AI assistant workflows, which is what made 600+ prototype interactions feasible to build and put in front of stakeholders.

Appointments & Advice

Services grouped into clear sections, with College vs. Major/Minor advising compared side by side and special cases surfaced upfront.

Virtual Front Desk

Two overlapping drop-in services consolidated into one, leaving students with a clearer choice between quick help and a scheduled appointment.

Routing Flow

A guided flow asks students about their situation and recommends the appropriate advising path instead of asking them to choose a service themselves.
