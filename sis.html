01. OVERVIEW

Problem

Students had to understand how UC Berkeley's advising is structured before they could get help from it.

Advising options sat in dropdown sections students opened one at a time. The difference between College and Major/Minor advisers was never stated. Requirements that determine whether a request can even proceed were buried near the bottom of the page.

Solution

Three pages, rebuilt around how students actually arrive.

A research-led redesign of three advising pages and a routing flow, built for a platform that couldn't support a single interactive element. Rather than explaining the college's structure more clearly, the redesign removes the need for students to understand it.

Appointments & Advice

Every Distinction Stated Outright

Advising options grouped under labeled sections, with a side-by-side comparison of College and Major/Minor advising and a table surfacing the requirements students used to miss.

Virtual Front Desk

One Service Instead of Two

Two drop-in services that used the same form and the same Zoom link, merged into one and compared against the choice students can actually make: a quick question now, or a scheduled appointment.
